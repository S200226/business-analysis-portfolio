📂 BPMN Process Modeling

This section contains selected BPMN diagrams that demonstrate my progress in learning and applying BPMN 2.0 to different business scenarios.

--

01. 📦 Luxxis Company – Order Fulfillment Process

A business process illustrating the end-to-end handling of a customer order, involving the Sales Manager, Accounting, and Warehouse Team, as well as interactions with the Customer and Supplier.
The process covers order validation, product availability verification, supplier interaction, invoicing, shipping, customer communication, and case completion.

--

02. 🏝️ Wonder Island – Permanent Visa Process

A process modeling the handling of a permanent visa application by the Wonder Island Embassy.
The process includes checking previous visa applications, conducting a background check, organizing a personal interview, handling application rejection, and coordinating visa printing with an external agency.

--

03. 🚀 Space Ship Manufacturing Process

A more complex manufacturing process involving Planning, Production, and Quality Assurance.
The model covers material availability, production planning, sample production and assessment, machine configuration, first-batch production, quality control, production performance evaluation, and customer feedback before launching the second production batch.

--

04. 🛂 Visa Process

A complex BPMN case covering several interconnected processes related to visa application handling and compliance.

The model includes visa document verification, communication with the police, processing applications for adult and underage applicants, visa production, application rejection and applicant blacklisting, database updates, and handling visa overstay cases.
