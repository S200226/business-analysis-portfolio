# 📊 Business Analysis Portfolio

Welcome to my Business Analysis Portfolio!

This repository documents my learning journey and practical development in Business Analysis, with a current focus on **business process modeling using BPMN 2.0**.

The portfolio will evolve alongside my skills and will gradually include projects related to process analysis, UML, requirements analysis, use cases, user stories, and other Business and IT Analysis techniques.



## 👩‍💻 About Me

Hi! I'm Joanna, an **Engineering Management** student at Gdańsk University of Technology (3rd year) with a growing interest in Business Analysis and IT.

I'm currently developing my skills in business process modeling and analysis, with a particular focus on BPMN 2.0. I created this portfolio to document my progress, apply theoretical knowledge through practical exercises, and showcase the projects I develop along the way.

My goal is to gradually expand my analytical toolkit by learning UML, requirements analysis, use cases, user stories, and other techniques used in Business and IT Analysis.
